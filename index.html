🔍 What's real in here

Crypto candles and scans come from Coinbase's public exchange feed. If it fails, Spellbook shows unavailable instead of fake values.

Historical Evidence Lab results are rule-based backtests with conservative assumptions; they do not include slippage, spread, fees or guaranteed fill quality.

Stocks are watchlist + education only in this build unless you connect a market-data source later. The app never places a brokerage order.
Educational trading journal + scanner. Not financial advice. Backtests and paper results can differ materially from live trading.
