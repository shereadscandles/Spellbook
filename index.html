      📜 How setups work

      A setup is one specific chart pattern that tends to move one way. You have 3. You don't hunt for them, the app does.
      • 🔮The app watches BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP & DOGE every 30 seconds for these 3 patterns.
      • ✨When one shows up you hear a sparkle, and it lands in Live Setups on Today and in Just Fired below.
      • 🚪You tap "Check it" and the Gate walks you through whether to take it. Most of the time the answer is "not this one," and that's fine.
      👉 Only learn Setup 1 for now. Ignore the other two until you've done 20 paper trades with it.

      ⚙️ Your Rules

      Pros usually risk 0.5–1% per trade and stop for the day at 2–3% down. Don't raise these after a losing day.

      Stocks + Alerts

      iPhone heads-up: alerts (sparkle sound + pop-ups) only work while the app is open on your screen. The phone pauses web apps in the background and when locked. Flip your ringer switch on or the sounds are muted.

      💾 Backup

      🔍 What's real in here

      Crypto prices, candles and setup scans: live from Coinbase's public feed (no key, works in the US). If the feed drops, the dot goes pink and nothing is faked.

      Stock quotes: live from Finnhub with your free key. Stock setups aren't auto-scanned (free candle data isn't available), so tap a stock to open its chart on TradingView.

      This app never places orders. Real mode = you click buy/sell in your broker, then log it here.
